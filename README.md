# 🚀 CareerLaunch

**CareerLaunch** is a student-focused career platform designed to help students explore job opportunities, internships, and improve their resumes for career growth.

> 🚧 **Project Status: Work in Progress (WIP)**
>
> CareerLaunch is currently under development. The frontend is being built using React, and additional features are planned for future iterations.

## 🎯 Project Vision

The goal of CareerLaunch is to create a platform where students can discover career opportunities and get guidance to improve their job readiness.

## ✨ Planned Features

- 🔍 **Job & Internship Discovery** – Explore opportunities relevant to students and freshers.
- 📄 **AI Resume Analyzer** – A planned tool to analyze resumes against job descriptions.
- 📊 **Resume Feedback** – Planned feedback on resume strengths and improvement areas.
- 👨‍🎓 **Student Dashboard** – A dedicated space for students to manage career-related activities.
- 🏢 **HR Dashboard** – A planned interface for recruiters to manage job listings and candidates.
- 🔐 **Authentication** – Planned login and registration functionality.
